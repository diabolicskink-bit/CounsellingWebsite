# Practice Direction

This owner-controlled brief defines Vive Counselling's stable practice scope
and positioning. It supplies facts and priorities for copy, design, research,
and implementation. It is not public copy and should not be paraphrased as if
it were.

## The practice

Vive Counselling is Joel Griffiths' counselling practice for adults. Joel is
based in Perth and works online with people across Australia.

The practice works with individuals, couples, and relationship configurations
involving more than two people, with practical arrangements discussed before
booking.

General counselling is central. Established areas of work include:

- anxiety and depression;
- trauma;
- relationship difficulties;
- attachment, shame, and self-criticism;
- grief and burnout; and
- sex, intimacy, and desire.

## Specialist knowledge and affirming practice

Joel has specialist knowledge of kink, BDSM, ethical non-monogamy, and
polyamory. LGBTQIA+ affirming counselling is also an established part of the
practice.

These strengths sit within a broad counselling practice and do not dictate the
focus of counselling. Public copy may state them where they help someone judge
fit, but should not turn possible enquiries into a recurring taxonomy or
reusable sentence formula.

Specialist-knowledge wording is a claim about Joel's professional knowledge
and counselling work, not an accreditation or a guarantee of universal fit.

## Approach and delivery

Joel works psychodynamically, with an attachment-informed and integrative
approach. This is part of how he works rather than the practice's defining
headline. Detailed methodology normally belongs on the Working with Joel page
or another place where it helps someone evaluate the work.

Online delivery, Perth location, fees, credentials, and availability are
practical eligibility, trust, and search facts. Use them where they answer a
real question; do not repeat them merely for emphasis or SEO.

## Positioning priorities

1. Present Vive as a broad adult counselling practice led by Joel Griffiths.
2. Let client concerns lead general Home and service copy; keep methodology in
   a supporting role unless it is the subject.
3. Make anxiety and depression, trauma, and relationship work easy to find.
4. State kink, non-monogamy, and LGBTQIA+ relevance clearly without turning
   identities, communities, or relationship structures into a list of client
   problems.
5. Make the available individual, couple, and more-than-two work clear where
   it affects fit or eligibility.

## Public-site purpose

The website should make an honest, persuasive case for Joel and the practice.
It should help suitable visitors understand the offer, judge whether Joel may
fit what they need, and take a clear next step.
