# Practice Direction

This owner-controlled brief defines Vive Counselling's stable practice scope
and positioning. It supplies facts and priorities for copy, design, research,
and implementation; it is not public copy.

## The Practice

Vive Counselling is Joel Griffiths' counselling practice for adults. Joel
works with:

- individuals;
- couples; and
- relationship configurations involving more than two people, with practical
  arrangements discussed before booking.

General counselling is central to the practice. Established areas of work
include:

- anxiety and depression;
- neurodivergence;
- trauma, including sexual assault and abuse;
- relationship difficulties;
- attachment, shame, and self-criticism;
- grief and burnout; and
- sex, intimacy, and desire.

Neurodiversity-related work is counselling rather than diagnostic assessment.

## Specialist Knowledge

Joel has specialist knowledge of kink, BDSM, ethical non-monogamy, and
polyamory. He works with these areas when they are:

- the reason someone is seeking counselling;
- relevant context for another concern; or
- simply an ordinary part of a person's life.

This is a claim about Joel's professional knowledge and counselling work, not
an accreditation or a guarantee of universal suitability.

LGBTQIA+ affirming counselling is also an established part of the practice.
Specialist and inclusion contexts are not presumed to be the cause of a
person's concern.

## Approach And Delivery

Joel works psychodynamically, with an attachment-informed and integrative
approach. This is part of how he works rather than the practice's defining
headline.

Joel is based in Perth and provides online counselling to adults across
Australia. Location and delivery are practical eligibility and search facts.

## Positioning Priorities

1. Present Vive as a broad adult counselling practice.
2. Make the available individual, couple, and more-than-two work clear where
   relevant.
3. State Joel's specialist kink and non-monogamy knowledge confidently on
   relevant pages.
4. Use approach information when it helps someone understand the work or
   decide whether Joel may suit them.
5. Use location, delivery, fees, and credentials where they answer practical,
   eligibility, trust, or search questions.

## Public-Site Purpose

The website should make an honest and persuasive case for Joel and the
practice. It should help suitable visitors understand what is offered, see why
Joel may be a good fit, and take a clear next step.

Public copy should be accurate and professionally responsible while still
being confident, engaging, and commercially effective.
