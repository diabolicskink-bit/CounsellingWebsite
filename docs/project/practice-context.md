## Areas of work

The practice provides general counselling. Areas of work include:

- anxiety and depression;
- attachment, shame, and self-criticism;
- grief and burnout;
- perfectionism and people-pleasing;
- relationship difficulties;
- sex, intimacy, and desire; and
- trauma, including CPTSD, sexual trauma, and the effects of single or repeated
  traumatic experiences.

## Joel's knowledge and experience

Joel has significant expertise in kink and BDSM, and specialist knowledge of
ethical non-monogamy and polyamory. LGBTQIA+ affirming counselling is also an
established part of the practice.

His work in these areas is informed by extensive lived experience within the
relevant communities.

Joel is committed to working with people who may have had difficulty finding
suitable therapy because their sexuality, gender, identity, relationships,
sex work, or diagnosis were misunderstood or treated as the problem.

Specialist knowledge here describes Joel's professional knowledge and
counselling work. It does not establish a separate accreditation or guarantee
that he will suit every client.

## Therapeutic approach

Joel works psychodynamically, with an attachment-informed and integrative
approach.
