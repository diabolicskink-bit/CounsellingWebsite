# Vive owner direction and taste

Read this reference for every Vive public-copy task. It records current owner
direction and observed failure mechanisms, not a complete house voice. Update
it only from explicit owner feedback or approved public wording.

## Current direction

- Home should speak primarily to client concerns and help a visitor judge fit.
- The website must sell Joel and the practice, not merely describe service
  scope, demonstrate inclusivity, or avoid making a wrong claim.
- Creative editorial judgement matters more than making the copy resemble an
  evidence-based paper. Research was gathered for content considerations, not
  to impose academic balance, cautious synthesis, or report-like prose.
- Therapeutic methodology is supporting material and normally belongs on the
  Working with Joel page rather than driving the first substantial Home
  section.
- The Home offer needs to make Joel Griffiths, anxiety and depression, trauma,
  relationships, and the practice's kink, ENM, and LGBTQIA+ relevance visible.
- Some development is useful in the first substantial section, but length must
  come from something worth saying rather than category expansion.

These are editorial requirements, not a list to reproduce in one sentence or
one paragraph per item.

## Explicitly rejected mechanisms

### Caseload wrapper

Rejected example: `People contact me about anxiety, depression, trauma and
relationship difficulties.`

Why it failed: it reports on an anonymous group from the provider's side and
uses a bland sentence to carry a coverage list. It does not bring the reader
closer to the problem or give the passage a thought.

Do not fix this by substituting `clients come to me`, `I help people with`, or
another provider-summary phrase. Change the proposition or grammatical centre.

### Published audience classification

Rejected example: `Any of these may be the reason for counselling, relevant
context, or simply part of your life.`

Why it failed: it turns an internal reader-range framework into polished public
copy. The neat comprehensive triad is the problem even though its distinctions
are defensible.

Do not reproduce this through synonyms such as central, peripheral,
background, incidental, ordinary, or unrelated. Use the analysis privately to
avoid a false assumption; publish a concrete service fact or omit it.

### Generic therapeutic completion

Rejected mechanisms include atmospheric openings such as `sometimes it is
clear`, vague unease such as `something is not right`, generic benefit language
such as `gives you room`, and methodology smuggled into Home through abstract
talk of patterns or earlier experiences.

Why they failed: they simulate recognition or therapeutic depth without enough
owner-specific material and tend to arrive in polished, balanced sequences.

## Positive voice status

No complete house-voice sample is approved yet. Do not infer one from existing
site copy or the owner's conversational typing. This is permission to exercise
strong professional creative judgement, not a reason to retreat into neutral
factual copy. Ask only when a missing fact or position would materially change
the practice being represented.
