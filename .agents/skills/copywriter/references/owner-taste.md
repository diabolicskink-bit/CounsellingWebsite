# Owner feedback and scoped decisions

This reference preserves feedback that may help with a relevant task. It does
not define a house voice or supply a brief for every page. Current writing
policy lives in `docs/project/writing-direction.md`; practice facts live in
`docs/project/practice-context.md` at the project root.

## September 2026 clarification

During the copywriter rebuild on 9 September 2026, the owner clarified that:

- Familiar or generic ideas are acceptable. The priority is good, natural
  copywriting, avoiding flowery, inflated or otherwise artificial prose.
- None of the site's copy is an ideal example. Even previously accepted copy
  retains some AI quality. Earlier approval for publication is not approval
  to use that passage as the target voice.
- The ENM, kink and LGBTQIA+ inclusion child pages still feel artificial.
  This is page-level feedback, not an explicit rejection of every sentence or
  rhetorical form on those pages.
- The writer should have substantial freedom by default, while respecting
  requests for narrower refinement as a professional copywriter would.
- The owner had not been actively managing the accumulated taste rules and
  authorised their reassessment in this rebuild.

The craft-review reference contains editorial diagnoses drawn from those
pages. Those diagnoses must not be recorded as additional owner prohibitions.

## Maintaining this record

Keep page composition decisions and individual draft revision notes with the
relevant task or page brief. Do not accumulate them here as standing writing
instructions. This reference is for feedback that remains useful across tasks.

Add feedback only from an explicit owner response. Record the passage or task,
what the owner actually accepted or rejected, and the scope of that decision.
Keep editorial interpretations labelled separately. An accepted revision does
not establish a universal rule or perfect example. Supersede conflicting
guidance when the owner changes direction; do not accumulate hidden rules from
each round of editing.
