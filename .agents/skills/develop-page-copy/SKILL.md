# Develop Page Copy

Use the `copywriter` skill to plan or write the requested amount of a page.
Keep the work proportional: a full page may be developed together, and a
single section does not require a page-governance system.

## Establish Context

For Vive Counselling, read:

1. `docs/project/practice-direction.md`;
2. `docs/project/writing-direction.md`;
3. the relevant page and surrounding copy; and
4. the `copywriter` skill.

Use current scope or source when exact operational details matter. Use research
only when the user requests it or a specific knowledge gap materially affects
the page.

Treat existing copy as context rather than an approved writing model. Treat
owner messages as direction rather than a voice sample.

## Match The Requested Deliverable

### Page Direction

When the user wants strategy or planning, provide a concise direction covering:

- the page's job and main message;
- the people and decisions it must serve;
- the strongest practice material to bring forward;
- a proposed sequence of sections, with one clear purpose for each;
- material that belongs elsewhere; and
- any decision that genuinely needs the owner's input.

Use descriptive planning labels rather than pretending they are finished
headings. Include alternatives only when they expose a consequential choice.
Build the main message from the service, concern, or concrete practice strength;
keep therapeutic approach in a supporting role unless the page is specifically
about how Joel works.

### Page Or Section Copy

When the user wants copy, write the requested scope: one passage, several
sections, or a complete page. A separate page plan is optional.

Use the copywriter workflow to produce one coherent recommendation. Check the
whole page for:

- a clear opening and useful progression;
- repetition between sections;
- enough practical and decision-making information;
- a specific positive case for Joel or the service;
- natural headings and calls to action; and
- an ending that leads somewhere useful without manufactured reassurance.

Label unapproved wording as recommended copy. Do not turn the result into a
record of drafts, critiques, or research.

### Durable Artifact

Create a file under `docs/page-plan/` only when the user requests a saved
artifact or when later design or implementation clearly needs one. Choose a
plain filename and include:

- the page and status;
- the selected direction or recommended copy;
- consequential assumptions or unresolved facts; and
- brief notes needed for the next stage.

Add semantic or interaction notes only where later design could otherwise
misread the content. Stable IDs, cross-links, coverage maps, and unit-by-unit
composition records are optional tools, not routine requirements.

## Boundaries

Page-copy work does not authorise visual design or public implementation unless
the user asks for those changes. It may recommend structure and semantic form
without prescribing cards, grids, styling, or components.

Preserve exact approved wording. Flag or omit unsupported practice details.
Keep specialist contexts accurate and non-pathologising.

Deliver the most useful artifact for the request, then stop.
